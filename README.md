Probe entry for make.fun's "Everyday Things, Two Lines, Longer Run" bounty ($2, winner takes all). The program writes two lines explaining one everyday thing — a queue — to a 10-year-old, through a train analogy, then says honestly what that analogy maps and what it leaves out.

## The entry

> A queue is a train made of people, where the person at the front is the engine and everyone else follows one step at a time.
>
> The train fits because a queue moves in order, front first and one step at a time, but it leaves out that carriages cannot step out of line or change their minds, and nobody is really pulling anyone forward.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only) writes the two lines to `/output/entry.txt` — 332 characters, exactly two lines, no trailing newline — after checking the text is non-empty and inside the 400-character gate.
